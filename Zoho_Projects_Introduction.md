**Zoho Projects** is a cloud-based project management tool designed to help teams plan, collaborate, and execute projects efficiently. It provides a range of features that facilitate project tracking, resource management, and collaboration among team members, enabling organizations to achieve their project goals on time.

### Key Features of Zoho Projects:

1. **Task Management**:
   - Create, assign, and track tasks easily with features like sub-tasks, task dependencies, and priorities. Users can set deadlines and receive notifications for upcoming tasks.

2. **Time Tracking**:
   - Log time spent on tasks with built-in timers and timesheets, allowing teams to monitor productivity and manage budgets effectively.

3. **Gantt Charts**:
   - Visualize project timelines and dependencies through Gantt charts, which help in planning and scheduling project phases.

4. **Collaboration Tools**:
   - Utilize discussion forums, document sharing, and comments to enhance team collaboration. Team members can communicate in real-time to keep everyone aligned.

5. **Milestones and Goals**:
   - Set milestones and goals for projects to monitor progress and ensure that teams stay on track towards achieving objectives.

6. **Reports and Analytics**:
   - Generate reports and dashboards to analyze project performance, resource utilization, and overall productivity, helping teams make informed decisions.

7. **Integrations**:
   - Integrate with other Zoho applications (like Zoho CRM and Zoho Invoice) and third-party tools (such as Slack and Google Drive) to streamline workflows.

8. **Mobile App**:
   - Access project details and updates on the go through the Zoho Projects mobile app, ensuring that team members can collaborate anytime, anywhere.

9. **Custom Workflows**:
   - Create custom workflows that fit the specific needs of your projects, automating repetitive tasks and approvals.

10. **Resource Management**:
    - Manage resources effectively by assigning them to tasks, tracking availability, and ensuring optimal utilization.
