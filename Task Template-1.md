Here's a **Task Template for Manufacturing and Supplying a Product** with tasks, sub-tasks, and suggested durations. The process is designed to cover everything from product design and manufacturing to shipping. 

### **Task List: Manufacturing and Supplying a Product**

---

### **Task 1: Product Design & Development** *(Total: 15 days)*
   - **Sub-task 1.1: Product Conceptualization** – *3 days*
     - Brainstorm product ideas and features.
     - Review customer requirements or market needs.
   - **Sub-task 1.2: Create Technical Specifications** – *4 days*
     - Define the product’s materials, components, and dimensions.
     - Prepare a detailed technical drawing or CAD model.
   - **Sub-task 1.3: Prototype Development** – *5 days*
     - Build and test a prototype.
     - Adjust design based on feedback and test results.
   - **Sub-task 1.4: Finalize Product Design** – *3 days*
     - Incorporate prototype feedback and finalize the design for manufacturing.

---

### **Task 2: Material Sourcing & Procurement** *(Total: 10 days)*
   - **Sub-task 2.1: Identify Suppliers** – *3 days*
     - Research and contact potential suppliers.
     - Obtain quotes for materials and negotiate prices.
   - **Sub-task 2.2: Finalize Purchase Orders** – *2 days*
     - Review supplier quotes and select the best option.
     - Place orders for materials and components.
   - **Sub-task 2.3: Arrange Material Delivery** – *5 days*
     - Schedule delivery of materials and track progress.
     - Inspect materials upon arrival for quality control.

---

### **Task 3: Production Planning** *(Total: 8 days)*
   - **Sub-task 3.1: Create Production Schedule** – *3 days*
     - Define timelines for each production stage.
     - Allocate resources (machines, manpower) for production.
   - **Sub-task 3.2: Allocate Resources** – *2 days*
     - Ensure that all necessary machines and tools are ready.
     - Assign team members to specific tasks or machines.
   - **Sub-task 3.3: Prepare for Production** – *3 days*
     - Set up machinery and production lines.
     - Conduct initial quality checks before starting production.

---

### **Task 4: Manufacturing Execution** *(Total: 15 days)*
   - **Sub-task 4.1: Begin Production** – *10 days*
     - Start the production process as per the schedule.
     - Track progress and resolve any issues during manufacturing.
   - **Sub-task 4.2: Quality Assurance (QA) Checks** – *3 days*
     - Inspect product quality during and after production.
     - Fix any defects or inconsistencies.
   - **Sub-task 4.3: Final Assembly & Packaging** – *2 days*
     - Assemble the product (if required) and package it for shipping.

---

### **Task 5: Inventory Management & Storage** *(Total: 6 days)*
   - **Sub-task 5.1: Store Finished Products** – *2 days*
     - Move finished products to storage or the warehouse.
     - Ensure proper labelling and organization for easy retrieval.
   - **Sub-task 5.2: Update Inventory System** – *2 days*
     - Log finished products in the inventory management system.
     - Track stock levels to ensure sufficient quantities for supply.
   - **Sub-task 5.3: Monitor Inventory Levels** – *2 days*
     - Regularly check inventory levels and plan for restocking if needed.

---

### **Task 6: Order Fulfilment & Logistics** *(Total: 8 days)*
   - **Sub-task 6.1: Receive and Process Orders** – *2 days*
     - Receive purchase orders from customers.
     - Verify order details and prepare for dispatch.
   - **Sub-task 6.2: Arrange Shipping/Transport** – *3 days*
     - Coordinate with shipping providers or logistics partners.
     - Prepare products for shipment (labeling, documentation, etc.).
   - **Sub-task 6.3: Track Shipment** – *3 days*
     - Monitor shipments until they reach the customer.
     - Address any delays or issues with logistics.

---

### **Task 7: Post-Delivery Support & Feedback** *(Total: 5 days)*
   - **Sub-task 7.1: Collect Customer Feedback** – *2 days*
     - Follow up with customers to collect feedback on the product and delivery.
   - **Sub-task 7.2: Address Customer Issues** – *2 days*
     - Handle any issues or defects reported by the customer.
   - **Sub-task 7.3: Continuous Improvement** – *1 day*
     - Use feedback to improve future product batches or processes.

---

### **Total Duration: 67 days**
